2. What Sparkle is

Sparkle is an online marketplace that connects people who need cleaning (“clients”) with independent cleaning professionals (“cleaners”). Sparkle provides the platform — job posting, quotes, booking, messaging, payments, photos, location check-ins and dispute review — but does not itself perform cleaning services.

Cleaners are independent businesses. They are not employees, agents or partners of Sparkle, and they decide whether to quote on a job, what to charge, and how to do the work. Clients choose which cleaner to book.

4. Posting, quoting and booking

  • Clients post a job describing the address, date and type of cleaning.
  • Cleaners may send a quote and message. A client has 48 hours to choose a quote before quotes expire.
  • When a client chooses a quote, the job is booked with that cleaner at that price. Job details should be accurate — a cleaner may decline or adjust if the job is materially different from what was described.

5. Fees and payments

  • Clients pay the cleaner's quote plus a Sparkle booking fee — currently 8%, or 10% for business accounts — and any optional add-ons they select. The total is shown before you book.
  • Cleaners pay a Sparkle success fee — currently 10% of the chosen quote — which is deducted from their earnings for that job.
  • Payments are processed by Stripe. A client's payment method may be authorized when a job is booked and is charged when the job is completed.
  • A cleaner's earnings for a job become available to cash out once the job is completed with its required photos. Standard payouts are free and usually arrive within 1–3 business days. Instant payouts, where available, carry a fee that is shown before you confirm.
  • Sparkle may change its fees. Changes don't affect jobs already booked, and we'll show current fees in the app before you confirm.
  • Cleaners are responsible for their own taxes, licenses and insurance.

6. Doing a job (cleaners)

  • Proof photos: you must upload at least one photo before you start and one after you finish to complete a job and be paid. Photos should show the work — not people — and must not be used for anything other than the job.
  • Location: tapping “On my way” shares your device's location with the client until you arrive, and Sparkle records your location when you arrive, while you're on site with the app open, when you finish and when you charge a lockout fee. See the Privacy Policy. You can decline location access, but the client won't see you approaching.
  • Arrive at the booked time, do the work you quoted for, and treat clients' homes and belongings with care.

7. Being booked (clients)

  • Describe the job accurately, including pets and anything a cleaner should know.
  • Make sure the cleaner can get in at the booked time, and keep the home safe to work in.
  • If you need to cancel, cancel in the app before the cleaner arrives.

8. Lockout fees

Cleaners may set a lockout fee, which is shown on their profile before you book. A lockout fee may be charged only when the cleaner arrived at the booked time, could not get in, waited their stated grace period, tried to contact the client, and the client did not cancel beforehand. The cleaner must confirm each of these and take a photo at the door. A client may dispute a lockout fee within 72 hours of it being charged.

9. Cancellations

A client or a cleaner can cancel a booked job in the app before the job starts. When a booking is cancelled, any payment authorization for it is released. Repeated late cancellations or no-shows may lead to account restrictions.

10. Problems, disputes and refunds

  • If something isn't right, a client can report a problem in the app within 72 hours of the job being completed, and may add photos.
  • While a report is open, the cleaner's earnings for that job are on hold. The cleaner can respond and add photos.
  • Sparkle reviews the report, the cleaner's before and after photos, messages, and the job's arrival and location records, and decides the outcome. Both people are told the decision and the reason.
  • If Sparkle decides in the client's favor, the client may be refunded to their original payment method, and the cleaner doesn't receive earnings for that job. If the cleaner has already cashed out those earnings, the amount is deducted from their future earnings.
  • Sparkle's decision settles the matter on the platform. It doesn't limit any legal rights you have.
