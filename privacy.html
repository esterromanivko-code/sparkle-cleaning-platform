1. Who we are

Sparkle (“Sparkle”, “we”, “us”) operates the Sparkle website and app, a marketplace that connects people who need cleaning (“clients”) with independent cleaning professionals (“cleaners”). This policy explains what information we collect when you use Sparkle, how we use and share it, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address, password (stored only in scrambled, hashed form), and optionally your phone number and city or ZIP code.
  • Profile: an optional profile photo. Cleaners also provide an hourly rate, the services they offer, their lockout fee policy and an optional bio.
  • Verification documents (cleaners): business license and certificate of insurance files, with details such as issuer, policy number and expiry date. If Sparkle offers background checks, the information needed for that check is collected by the screening provider.
  • Jobs: the service address, date, type of cleaning, home details and notes a client enters, and the quotes and messages cleaners send.
  • Photos: before and after photos cleaners take of their work, photos taken at the door when a lockout fee is charged, and photos either person attaches to a problem report. When a photo is uploaded we remove its embedded data, including the GPS location and camera details stored inside the image file.
  • Communications: in-app messages, reviews, problem reports and responses, and support requests.

Information created when you use Sparkle

  • Payments: payments and payouts are processed by Stripe. We receive payment status, amounts and Stripe reference numbers, and may see a card's brand and last four digits. We never receive or store full card numbers. Cleaners' bank or debit card details for payouts are held by Stripe.
  • Earnings and bookings: booking status, completion times, fees, earnings, cashouts, refunds and dispute decisions.
  • Location (cleaners): described in section 3.
  • Device and security information: IP address, browser and device type, and sign-in activity, which we use to keep accounts secure and to prevent abuse.

3. Location

Sparkle uses location only for cleaners, and only when the cleaner allows location access on their device. We do not collect clients' device location.

  • On the way: when a cleaner taps “On my way”, their location is sent while the Sparkle app is open, so the client can see them approaching and receive “on the way” and “almost there” notifications. The client can see the cleaner's live location only until the cleaner arrives.
  • Check-ins: Sparkle records the cleaner's location when they tap “I've arrived”, from time to time while the job is in progress and the app is open, when they complete the job, and when they charge a lockout fee. We compare these points with the job's address to record arrival time, time on site and how far the check-in was from the address. This record helps resolve disputes about whether and how long a cleaner was at a home.
  • Address coordinates: to measure those distances, we convert a job's address into map coordinates using a geocoding service (see section 5).

Cleaners can decline or turn off location access at any time in their device or browser settings. Sparkle still works without it, but clients won't see the cleaner approaching and check-ins won't include a location.

4. How we use information

  • To run the marketplace: creating accounts, posting jobs, sending quotes, booking, messaging and reviews.
  • To process payments, payouts, refunds and fees.
  • To show clients when a cleaner is on the way and has arrived, and to keep a record of each visit.
  • To review verification documents and display verification badges.
  • To investigate problem reports and lockout fee disputes, using photos, messages and location records.
  • To keep Sparkle safe: preventing fraud, spam and abuse, securing accounts, and enforcing our Terms of Service.
  • To send service notifications and respond to support requests.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

5. How information is shared

Between clients and cleaners

  • Cleaners who can quote on an open job see its address, date and details. The cleaner who is booked also sees the client's first name and messages.
  • Clients see cleaners' first name and last initial, profile photo, rating and reviews, rate, services, lockout policy and verification badges.
  • A job's photos are visible only to that job's client, its cleaner and Sparkle staff.
  • While a cleaner is on the way, the client sees the cleaner's live location. Afterwards the client sees the arrival time and time on site.

Service providers

We use trusted companies to run Sparkle. They may process information only to provide their service to us:

  • Stripe — card payments, refunds and cleaner payouts.
  • Cloudflare — Turnstile bot protection on sign-in and sign-up.
  • Google — maps shown in the app, and web fonts.
  • U.S. Census Bureau Geocoder — converting job addresses into map coordinates.
  • Railway — hosting for our servers, database and uploaded files.
  • Netlify — hosting for our website.
  • Our email delivery provider — account and support emails.
  • Background check providers — only if and when Sparkle offers background checks.

Other sharing

  • Legal and safety: when we believe in good faith that it's required by law or legal process, or needed to protect the safety, rights or property of our users, the public or Sparkle.
  • Business changes: if Sparkle is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

Sparkle does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

  • The app stores sign-in tokens and a few preferences (such as notification settings) in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. Signing out clears your sign-in tokens.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile, which protects sign-in and sign-up from bots, and Google Maps, when a map is shown, may set their own cookies or similar storage to work. Their use is covered by Cloudflare's and Google's privacy policies.

You can clear cookies and local storage in your browser settings. If you do, you'll be signed out.

7. How long we keep information

  • Account and profile information: while your account is open. When you delete your account it is removed as described in section 8.
  • Job photos and precise location history: deleted automatically 180 days after the job ends, unless a dispute about that job is still open or we are legally required to keep them. Arrival times, time on site and check-in distances are kept with the job record.
  • Bookings, payments, payouts, fees, refunds and dispute decisions: kept as long as needed for tax, accounting and legal purposes, generally up to seven years.
  • Backups: we keep rolling backups of our database. Deleted information is removed from them as older backups expire, generally within five weeks.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Update your information: in the app, under Settings and My profile.
  • Location: allow or block location access in your device or browser settings.
  • Delete your account: go to Settings → Delete account. You'll need to finish or cancel any upcoming jobs, cash out any earnings, and wait for open problem reports to be resolved first. Deleting your account removes your name, email, phone number, profile photo, saved address, bio, services, verification documents, mileage logs, notifications and sign-in sessions. Records that other people or the law depend on — past jobs, payments, payouts, reviews and messages — are kept with your name replaced by “Deleted user”. Job photos and location history follow the 180-day schedule above.
  • Requests: depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to ask how it's used. Send a request through Contact support in the app (or the contact form on our website) and we'll respond as the law requires. We won't treat you differently for exercising these rights.

9. Security

We protect information with encryption in transit (HTTPS), hashed passwords, access controls that limit job photos and verification documents to the people who need them, and monitoring for abuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your information and will notify you of a breach as required by law.

10. Children

Sparkle is only for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us and we'll delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Sparkle changes. We'll change the effective date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app or by email before they take effect.

12. Contact us

Questions or requests about your privacy? In the app, go to Settings → Contact support, or use the contact form on the Sparkle website.
