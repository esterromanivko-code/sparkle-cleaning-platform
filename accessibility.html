Accessibility

Updated September 15, 2026

Everyone should be able to book a clean or run a cleaning business on Sparkle. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA across our website and app.

What we do

  • Label buttons, form fields and dialogs so screen readers can announce them.
  • Support keyboard navigation, including closing dialogs with the Escape key.
  • Keep text readable with sufficient color contrast, and never rely on color alone to show status.
  • Design pages to work on phones, tablets and computers, and with browser zoom.

Known limitations

  • Maps showing a cleaner's approach may be hard to use with a screen reader. The same information — whether your cleaner is on the way, roughly how far away they are, and when they arrived — is always shown as text too.
  • Photos uploaded by cleaners and clients don't have written descriptions.

Tell us about a barrier

If something on Sparkle is hard to use, we want to fix it. In the app, go to Settings → Contact support, or use the contact form on the Sparkle website. Tell us the page and what happened, and we'll get back to you.
